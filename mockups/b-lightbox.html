Why we exist

A 22-minute episode takes about 1,875 artist-hours, and half of them go to the drawings between the keys. Studios can't hire animators fast enough, so seasons take years. We're building the tool that gives that time back, without taking the pencil out of the animator's hand.

How it works

  1. 01Your keys

    Load your key drawings as vector line art (SVG or Grease Pencil), with your exposure sheet. Holds and timing stay yours.

  2. 02Our drafts

    Our model matches each line between the keys and moves it along the motion, on the frames your sheet calls for.

  3. 03Your call

    Scrub with onion skin. Accept drawing by drawing, retake a single line, or fix it and lock it. Locked drawings are never touched.

Your timing stays yours.

We draw on the frames your exposure sheet asks for. Holds, spacing and favours are the animator's decisions, not ours.

Every line stays editable.

Output is vector, not pixels. Nobody redraws a frame to make a director's note.

Your art stays in your building.

The model runs on your own machines, and your drawings are never used for training without your permission.
