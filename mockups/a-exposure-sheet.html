Sc. 001 — In-betweening for 2D animation

Draw the keys.
We draw the in‑betweens.

Rabbit Labs drafts the drawings between your animators' keys, on their timing, as clean vector lines they can edit. They keep what works and retake the rest.

Pilot it on your shot founders@rabbitlab.ai

01Why

Every second of 2D animation is 12 to 24 drawings. Animators draw the keys. Every drawing between them is still drawn by hand. We give that time back.

02How it works

Your keys and your timing go in. Editable lines come out.

StepWhat happensWhat stays yours
01Import keysLoad your key drawings as vector line art (SVG or Grease Pencil), with your exposure sheet.Your keys and your timing. Holds stay where you put them.
02GenerateOur model matches each line between the keys and moves it along the motion, on the frames your sheet calls for.Every line is vector, editable in your pipeline.
03ReviewScrub with onion skin. Accept drawing by drawing, retake a single line, or fix it by hand and lock it.The final say. Locked drawings are never touched.

Runs on your own machines. Your art never leaves the building, and it is never used for training without your permission.
