My pick: B, with A's exposure-sheet demo as the option to borrow. Its colour coding (graphite for your keys, blue for our drafts) explains the product before anyone reads a word, and "without taking the pencil out of the animator's hand" is the motive in one line. C is the strongest for investors. A is the most original, but it asks the most of a visitor.

Rules all three follow

  • No gradients, glass, glows, pill badges, emoji or icon-in-a-rounded-square grids.
  • No Inter and no purple. Each direction pairs a distinctive face with a mono or grotesk and uses at most one accent colour.
  • The look comes from real animation tools: exposure sheets, peg holes, keys and holds, onion skin, timecode.
  • The demo is real vector drawing: 2 keys and 10 in-betweens computed on the animator's timing (slow in and slow out, ears trailing the head). It's labelled as an illustration, not model output.
  • Claims stay honest: every number gives its n and says what "pass" means.
